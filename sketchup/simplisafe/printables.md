# SimpliSafe Entry Sensor Covers — Printables Listing

## Short Description (120 chars)

3D printed covers for SimpliSafe entry sensors — swap the plain white for black, wood, or any color to match your frame.

## Full Description

```html
<p>SimpliSafe only sells their entry sensors in white, which looks out of place on dark window and door frames. These covers
slide over both pieces of the 2-part entry sensor — the active side (with the status LED and reset button) and the passive
magnetic side — letting you print them in any color or filament finish you like.</p>

<p>Designed for a snug but removable fit: the covers slip on by hand, stay put during normal use, and come off cleanly if you
ever need to access the sensor. A pill-shaped hole on the active-side cover lines up with the status LED. No hole is
included for the reset button — the fit is snug enough that pressing through the wall isn't practical anyway. Just pull
the cover off to reset and slide it back on; no adhesive needed.</p>

<h3>Print Settings</h3>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Material:</strong> PLA or PETG (PETG recommended for longevity near windows in direct sun)</li>
  <li><strong>Layer height:</strong> 0.2 mm</li>
  <li><strong>Perimeters / walls:</strong> 2 (0.8 mm at 0.4 mm nozzle)</li>
    <li><strong>Infill:</strong> None</li>

  <li><strong>Supports:</strong> None required</li>
  <li><strong>Orientation:</strong> Open end down (flat on the bed)</li>
</ul>

<h3>Fit Notes</h3>

<p>The interior is sized with 0.3 mm per side clearance over the sensor body. If your printer runs tight, scale X/Y by
101–102% or re-slice with slightly more horizontal expansion. If it's too loose, try 99%.</p>
```
