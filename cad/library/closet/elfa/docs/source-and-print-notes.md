# Elfa wire shelf hooks

Sliding hooks that clip onto the Container Store Elfa wire shelves, by Brad on Printables (model 1118038, published 2025-01-31). Public domain (CC0): no attribution required, remixing and commercial use allowed. The download page is saved as the PDF beside this file; the untouched download is in `originals/`.

These are kept for their latch: the clip that grips the shelf wire and lets the hook slide forward and back. Every mesh shares the same latch, 38.7 mm wide, so any of them can donate it.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `slidinghook.stl` | Tall J hook, 101 mm, from `closethookv2.3mf` |
| `hanger_hook.stl` | Short hook with a limited reach, 59 mm, from `closethookv2.3mf`; low-poly (2,668 triangles), the cleanest latch to trace |
| `slide10mmhook.stl` | Flat 101 mm arm with a 10 mm lip, from `twomorehooks.3mf` |
| `hookpoleholder.stl` | 131 mm arm ending in an open clamp for a pole, from `twomorehooks.3mf` |
| `closethookv2.3mf` | Upstream project, three hooks; the long one with interior holes was not exported |
| `twomorehooks.3mf` | Upstream project, re-saved in Flash Studio on 2026-10-17; the zip holds the original |

Upstream printed them in PLA with supports, 0.6 mm nozzle at 0.32 mm layers, 57 g for the three-hook plate. Not tested on the AD5M Pro.
