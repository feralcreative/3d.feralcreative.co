Many thanks to 3dptrk on printables.com who's design was the inspiration for my design. Storage stand for the Fanttik X200 vacuum/inflator and its accessories. No supports required. I printed mine on an Ender3 S1 in PETG with a 0.16mm layer height, but any material and layer height should be fine.
